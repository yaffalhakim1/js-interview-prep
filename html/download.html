Check yourself

Why does the PDF show the answers?

Because it is meant for reading, not for testing yourself. If you want to test yourself, use the self-check boxes on the website: the answer is hidden until you click.

Why keep three formats instead of one?

They are good at different things. The website can run the code. The PDF works offline and prints. Markdown is searchable and easy to copy from. Since all three come from one source, keeping them costs nothing.
